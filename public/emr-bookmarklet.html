Send EMR page to the app

1. Show your browser's bookmarks bar, then drag this button onto it:

Send to Drug Chart

2. Open a patient in the hospital EMR (the page with the header and notes).

3. Click the bookmark. The app opens with the patient's details filled in (or opens the patient if already registered). Then in the drug chart, Bulk Upload Drugs is prefilled with the orders. Always review before saving.

If nothing happens: allow pop-ups for the EMR site. If the page still can't be read, the EMR may show its content inside a frame from another address. Tell the developer.

The text goes directly from the EMR tab to this app in your browser. It is not sent to any other server.
